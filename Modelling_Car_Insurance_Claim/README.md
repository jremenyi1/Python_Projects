# Modeling Car Insurance Claim Outcomes

## Background information
Machine learning is widely used in the insurance industry, and has long been recognised as highly data-driven. In this project, I analyse customer data from the On the Road car insurance company to identify the features that produce the most accurate logistic regression model for predicting whether a claim will be made against a policy.

## Data
- raw data - car_insurance.csv

## Aim
- Identify the single feature of the data that is the **best predictor** of whether a customer will put in a **claim** (the "outcome" column), excluding the "id" column.
- Store as a DataFrame called best_feature_df, containing columns named "best_feature" and "best_accuracy" with the name of the feature with the highest accuracy, and the respective accuracy score.
